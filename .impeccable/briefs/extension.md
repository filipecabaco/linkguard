# Surface brief: LinkGuard extension UI (block page, click warning, popup, settings, welcome)

Mode: Operate

## Direction contract

THESIS: A link is an address; LinkGuard is the sorting office that stamps the bad ones and ticks the reason on the slip. Refuses the antivirus default (navy shield, green check, red "Threat blocked" card).

OWN-WORLD: Kraft band shell, thermal-white label content, iron ink, franking-red double-ruled stamp, yellow sticker tag for suspicious. Archivo condensed caps for stamps and headlines, mono addresses, system body.

STORY: The person sees one plain sentence saying what the link is doing, sees where it really goes, reads up to three ticked reasons, and goes back. The way through exists, but it's quiet.

FIRST VIEWPORT (block page): kraft band with mark. Sheet split about 58/42: left has the headline (Archivo 75% width, 64px), lede, address label with tilted NOT SAFE stamp overlapping its corner, and Go back as the only filled button bottom-left. Right has the "Why it was stopped" ruled slip. Stacks below 900px, with the slip under the label and Go back full-width.

FORM: Return to Sender (seed:98f1357c04e0cb0c, assigned). Raises: one stamp at every scale; colour commits whole regions; three-reason budget; three inks plus a state colour; one decision owns the screen; state by form, not hue alone.

SIGNATURE: the return slip (label, stamp, ticked reasons) reused in the click warning and popup rows. Motion: the stamp lands once.
